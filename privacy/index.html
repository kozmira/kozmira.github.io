Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Türkçe

Kozmira ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we collect

Kozmira collects no personal data, sends nothing to servers and shares nothing with third parties.

The information you enter in the app is stored only on your own device:

  • Your zodiac sign
  • Your date, time and place of birth and your name (if you enter them)
  • Another person's sign or birth details you enter for compatibility (if you enter them)
  • Your tarot readings: your questions, the cards drawn and their interpretations (reading history)
  • Your notification preferences and other app settings

This information is used to calculate your horoscopes, birth chart, transits and compatibility on your device. All calculations happen on your device; the app does not need an internet connection to work.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily notification, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No server or device identifier is used to send them.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Kozmira shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your information at any time in the app under Settings > About > Delete all my data. Removing the app from your device also deletes all your information.

Children

Kozmira is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

You can send your questions to kozmiraapp@gmail.com.
