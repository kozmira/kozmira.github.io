Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Türkçe

Riduna ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we collect

Riduna collects no personal data, sends nothing to servers and shares nothing with third parties. The only exception is the anonymous error reports described below.

The information you enter in the app is stored only on your own device:

  • Your zodiac sign
  • Your date, time and place of birth and your name (if you enter them)
  • The names, signs or birth details of the people you save for compatibility (if you enter them)
  • Your tarot readings: your questions, the cards drawn, their interpretations and the notes you add to them (reading history)
  • Your notification preferences and other app settings

This information is used to calculate your horoscopes, birth chart, transits and compatibility on your device. All calculations happen on your device; the app does not need an internet connection to work.

If your phone's backup is turned on (iCloud or Google backup), this information is included in your phone's own backup and can be restored to a new phone. We have no access to these backups.

Error reports

When the app hits an unexpected error, the technical details of the error, the name of the screen where it happened and the app and operating system version are sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data stored in the EU) so we can fix it. Your name, birth details, people, notes, IP address and any device or install identifier are never included. If you turn off Settings > About > Send error reports, the app sends nothing at all.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily notification, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No server or device identifier is used to send them.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Riduna shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

YouTube

Riduna publishes its own short videos on its YouTube channel, @kozmiraapp. To upload them, our internal publishing tool uses the YouTube API Services, with permission given only by Riduna's own Google account. The tool uploads only videos we created and reads only our own channel's details (its name and recent uploads, so the same video is not uploaded twice). It does not access, collect, store or share any information about you or any other YouTube user, and the Riduna app itself does not connect to YouTube. Because no YouTube API data is stored, there is no such data to delete.

YouTube is a Google service. When you watch our videos on YouTube, the YouTube Terms of Service apply and Google processes your data under the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to any tool through YouTube API Services can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your information at any time in the app under Settings > About > Delete all my data. Removing the app from your device also deletes all your information.

Children

Riduna is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

You can send your questions to info@ridunaapp.com.
